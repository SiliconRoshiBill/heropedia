Most people use AI the same way: they ask a question and get an answer. An office hour runs the
other way round. A hero asks *you* the questions, one at a time, pushes back when an answer is
vague, and ends with one thing to do by Friday.

Heropedia's [Elon Musk persona](/founder/ceo-advisor/elon-musk) is built for exactly that. It is an
AI persona based on Musk's public interviews and the books written about him, with every fact tied
to a source. It is not Elon Musk, and he has nothing to do with Heropedia.

## Start in under a minute

Pick whichever AI you already use:

- **ChatGPT, Gemini or any chat window.** Open the [Elon Musk page](/founder/ceo-advisor/elon-musk),
  press **Copy the Musk office-hour prompt**, paste it into a new chat and send. The persona is
  inside the prompt, so there is nothing to install.
- **Claude.** Add Heropedia once as a custom connector with the URL `https://www.heropedia.org/mcp`
  (Settings, then Connectors). After that, say *"Hold office hours with Elon Musk on my pricing"*
  in any chat. The [setup guide](/mcp-guide) walks through each step.
- **Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI.** Install the office-hour skill with the command on the hero
  page, then say *"Office hour with Elon Musk on my onboarding."* Your notes stay on your machine.

## The six questions he will ask

The persona works through six questions. Have rough answers ready and the session goes much faster.

1. **What is the requirement you are working to, and who exactly set it?** "The customer" or
   "legal" is not a name. He wants a person and their reason.
2. **What will you delete from the product or the process this week, and what breaks if you are
   wrong?** Name a step, feature, meeting or approval, not something cosmetic.
3. **What is the physical or economic limit here, and how far are you from it?** Two numbers side
   by side: the theoretical floor and today's actual figure. Competitors are not the benchmark.
4. **Which number tells you whether this is working, in what unit, and what is it today?** One
   metric, a current value and a dated target.
5. **How long does it take from deciding to change something to seeing the result, and what would
   halve that time?**
6. **What would have to be true for this to work at ten times today's scale?** He is looking for
   the single bottleneck that breaks first, with a number.

These follow the order of the five-step process documented in the persona's sources: question every
requirement, delete, simplify and optimize, accelerate cycle time, and only then automate.

## How to get a useful session

- **Bring one real problem.** "My onboarding loses half of signups at step 4" works far better than
  "review my startup".
- **Answer with numbers.** The persona is written to push back on adjectives such as "faster" or
  "better" until you give it a unit and a value.
- **Push back once.** When it names what it thinks your real problem is, argue. The diagnosis gets
  sharper.
- **Write down the Friday action.** Every session ends with one specific thing to do this week and
  the way you are most likely to fool yourself. That line is the point of the exercise.
