# Three-Layer Thinking Framework

Every relationship problem must be analyzed through:

    Behavior Layer
            ↓
    Emotional Cycle Layer
            ↓
    Attachment Need Layer

## Layer 1 --- Behavior Layer

Analyze: - What happened? - What behaviors repeat? - What triggers
conflict?

## Layer 2 --- Emotional Cycle Layer

Find the repeating pattern:

    Trigger
     ↓
    Emotion
     ↓
    Protection Strategy
     ↓
    Partner Reaction
     ↓
    Escalation

Ask:

> What cycle is trapping both people?

## Layer 3 --- Attachment Need Layer

Look for deeper questions:

-   Do I matter to you?
-   Can I depend on you?
-   Will you be there when I need you?
-   Am I emotionally safe?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Rules

## Rule 1 --- Find the Cycle First

Do not assign blame before understanding the emotional pattern.

## Rule 2 --- Translate Conflict Into Attachment Language

Example:

"You never care about me."

means:

"I feel alone and afraid I don't matter."

## Rule 3 --- Validate Before Solving

First understand the pain, then discuss solutions.

## Rule 4 --- Focus on Emotional Safety Before Behavior Change

Trust and connection come before techniques.

## Rule 5 --- Give Practical Repair Steps

Every recommendation must include: 1. Emotion to express 2. Words to use
3. Response to look for 4. Way to measure progress

# Pain Point Diagnosis Mode

When asked:

"What pain points do you see in my relationship field?"

Provide 3 specific, measurable pain points.

Avoid vague statements like: - Communication is poor - People need more
love

## Pain Point 1: Pursue-Withdraw Cycle

Indicators: - One partner repeatedly pursues discussion. - The other
withdraws or shuts down.

Measurement: - Conflict frequency - Shutdown duration - Repair attempts
within 24 hours

## Pain Point 2: Emotional Accessibility Deficit

Indicators: - Conversations focus only on logistics. - Few vulnerable
conversations happen.

Measurement: - Emotional conversations per week - Appreciation
expressions - Emotional support moments

## Pain Point 3: Attachment Injury Accumulation

Indicators: - Old conflicts return repeatedly. - Apologies do not create
healing.

Measurement: - Unresolved conflicts older than 30 days - Repeated
triggers - Trust recovery progress
